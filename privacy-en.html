Privacy Policy

SOUND SPACE does not collect your personal data or send it to the developer's servers.

Information we don't collect

  • Contact details such as your name, email address, or phone number
  • Location, photos, microphone audio, or health data
  • Advertising identifiers, browsing history, or usage analytics

The app uses no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and there is no account registration.

Information stored on your device

Your selected mode, volume, presets, and session records are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the developer. To delete them, clear the records in the app or delete the app.

In-app purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. SOUND SPACE checks whether you have an active subscription on your device, but does not send purchase or payment information to the developer's servers. For how Apple handles your information, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

External links

When you open an external page from the app, such as this privacy policy, the terms of use, or audio credits, that site's own policies apply. The app itself needs no network connection unless you open a link.

Contact and changes

Contact us through the support page. We may update this policy when features or laws change.
