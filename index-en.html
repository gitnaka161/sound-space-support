QUICK START

Make three choices, then begin

Every added sound is optional. Start with just the music and layer other sounds later if you like.

  1. 1
    Choose music

    Pick a mode — Deep Meditation, Relax, Sleep, Focus, or Solfeggio — and a music track.

  2. 2
    Layer sounds

    Add only the sounds you want, such as rain, waves, a river, a forest, or a campfire.

  3. 3
    Set the time

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What if there's no sound?

Check your device volume, the Bluetooth output, and whether another app is playing audio. Then quit SOUND SPACE and open it again. Gamma 40 is mostly low frequencies, so listen with headphones or an external speaker.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap PLUS in the top-right corner of the app and choose Restore Purchases. If your Apple Account has an active purchase, your access will be restored.

How do I check or cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your Apple Account, tap Subscriptions, and choose SOUND SPACE. You can check or cancel your subscription there.

Are my records or settings sent anywhere?

No. Your settings and session records are stored on your device. The app uses no advertising, analytics, or tracking. Only purchases and restores use Apple's StoreKit.

Can Gamma 40 play together with meditation music?

No. Gamma 40 isn't music; it's a standalone tool that plays a fixed 40 Hz stimulus tone. It's used on its own tab, separate from meditation music.

Health and safety

SOUND SPACE is not a medical device and is not intended for diagnosis, treatment, prevention, or measuring effects. Avoid long listening at high volume, and stop using the app if you feel any discomfort. If you have a history of epilepsy, consult a doctor before use.
